# Attribution

CFDA TREC RAG 2026 was developed collaboratively by a team of contributors.
This repository presents the selected Retrieval and Agentic RAG
implementation, validation tooling, documentation, and canonical technical
report.

Itzel0115's work focused on retrieval-side experimentation and validation,
analysis of ranking and retrieval variants around the integrated system,
technical and reproducibility validation, final deliverable preparation, and
leading the drafting and iterative finalization of the technical report.

Relevant external components and conventions include Pyserini and the
ClimbMix retrieval service, TREC RAG task specifications and evaluation
conventions, Hugging Face/ONNX model families, external LLM services, and ACL
report and bibliography tooling. Their respective terms and licenses govern
their use.

Technical-report authorship and author order are preserved from the canonical
report artifact. This repository does not change that attribution.
