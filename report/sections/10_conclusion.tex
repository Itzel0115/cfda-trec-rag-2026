\section{Conclusion}

We developed separate Retrieval and RAG pipelines for TREC RAG 2026.
The Retrieval results show that a protected head can be combined with
deep-tail reranking while the variable-depth cutoff remains tied to
post-splice, pre-deep-CE candidate-pool scores. The RAG results show that coverage and citation
support must be examined together when comparing stored RAG
configurations.

Both pipelines separate protected or bounded evidence-selection stages
from later ranking and answer-construction stages: Retrieval protects the head before tail reranking, and RAG caps
additional reading before dense writing and verification. Further
experiments should compare adaptive stopping policies under controlled
reading budgets. Organizer judgments, when released, will be needed to
determine whether the development-set patterns transfer to the test
topics.
