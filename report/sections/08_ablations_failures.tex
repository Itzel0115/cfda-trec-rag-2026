\subsection{Development Variants and Failure Analysis}
\label{sec:ablations}

Query2Doc introduces pseudo-document vocabulary while the original
narrative remains an anchor. Facet routes expose separately searchable
requirements. To prevent facet-derived candidates from reordering the
pre-facet head, we retain the pre-facet top-200 prefix and incorporate
facet-derived candidates only into the complementary tail.

Recall@submitted-$k$ depends on the topic-specific submitted cutoff and
must therefore be evaluated on the submitted file. The development Retrieval
output spans 100--1,000 documents per topic; the corresponding official
119-topic outputs span 137--4,218. The fixed top-1000 development output provides
a fixed-depth comparison with Recall@1000=0.3159. The deep CE tail
preserves the 100-document head and changes tail ordering through the
specified fusion with pool order; the topic depth remains determined by
post-splice, pre-deep-CE candidate-pool scores.

The 12-document-cap baseline is descriptively lower than both
60-document configurations in Table~\ref{tab:rag-results}. The selected
bounded 60-document acquisition and 60-document deep-reading
configurations form the close pair;
their $V_{\mathrm{strict}}$ difference is inconclusive, and selection reflects stronger
citation-support behavior rather than a statistically significant
$V_{\mathrm{strict}}$ advantage. A
larger-budget extended-reading configuration encountered server errors
on most generation calls, while its recorded failure status did not
reliably surface those failures. Because execution was incomplete and
failure reporting was unreliable, we exclude it from formal comparison
in Table~\ref{tab:rag-results}.

The final word-limit cap enforces the 1,024-word output contract, which
the selected RAG output satisfies; it does not establish that trimming
improves answer semantics.
