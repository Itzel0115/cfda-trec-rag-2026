\begin{abstract}
We developed separate CFDA pipelines for the Retrieval (R) and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) tasks of TREC RAG 2026. The Retrieval pipeline combines multiple query routes, protected-head reranking, cross-encoder-guided deep-tail reranking, and variable-depth serialization. The RAG pipeline uses a bounded evidence-acquisition loop followed by dense answer writing, sentence-level verification, and citation ordering. On the 22-topic development set, the selected Retrieval output obtains nDCG@10 of 0.7456, Recall@submitted-$k$ of 0.2343, P@10 of 0.9076, MAP of 0.1432, and MRR of 0.9848. The selected RAG output obtains $V_{\mathrm{strict}}=0.4656$, $A=0.5298$, FS $=97.0\%$, and NS $=0.1\%$. All effectiveness results in this report come from the development set; organizer test-set effectiveness results were unavailable at the time of writing.
\end{abstract}
