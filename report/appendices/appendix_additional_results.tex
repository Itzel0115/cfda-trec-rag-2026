\section{Follow-up Query Constraints and Validation}
\label{app:query-legality}

The validator lowercases and extracts \texttt{[a-z0-9-]+} tokens,
requiring 4--12. It rejects a normalized duplicate of any prior query
(including the anchor) or earlier accepted proposal in the iteration;
any \texttt{?}, brace, period, semicolon, colon, or triple-backtick
fence; and tokens equal to: obtain, find, source, sources, detail,
detailed, comprehensive, concrete, examples, evidence, information,
overview, history, impact, provide, directly, addresses, address,
account, explain, or the token \emph{and}. It also rejects an anchor-overlap ratio above
0.75, computed as the number of query-token occurrences whose token
also appears in the anchor
divided by the number of distinct tokens in their union. At most the
first three valid proposals are retained. A parse-invalid judge response
triggers retries for up to five total attempts; a parsed insufficient response with no
valid proposal stops the loop with no valid follow-up query.
